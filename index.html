Frequently Asked Questions

        

            
        

            

                 How does Color Master generate harmonious color palettes?
            

            
Color Master uses color harmony algorithms based on the standard 360-degree color wheel, calculating exact angular offsets for Monochromatic, Analogous, Complementary, and Triadic color harmonies.

        

  
        

            

                 Is Color Master completely free and private to use?
            

            
Yes. Color Master is 100% free with no account registration required. All color calculations, canvas pixel sampling, and conversions run directly inside your browser client-side with zero data sent to external servers.

        

  
        

            

                 How do I convert color palettes to CSS, Flutter, or SwiftUI code?
            

            
Click the "Select" icon on any color swatch to add it to your selection bar, then click "Convert Selected". The Universal Converter instantly produces ready-to-copy code snippets for CSS Custom Properties, Flutter Color arrays, SwiftUI Color structs, and Android XML color resources.

        

  
        

            

                 Can I extract color codes directly from an image or screenshot?
            

            
Yes. In the Image Color Extractor & Picker section, upload any PNG, JPG, or WEBP image. The tool automatically detects 5 dominant palette colors and provides an interactive magnifying loupe allowing you to click anywhere on the image to sample exact pixel HEX codes.
